// OVER-THE-SHOULDER MOTION BOXING // HARD MODE //

MOTION
BOXER

Your head is the circle. Your hands roam the whole screen. Keep a hand near your own head, steady and unstretched, to parry — get parried and that hand is stunned for 2 seconds.

01Allow camera — head and hands need to be visible
02Sway left/right to move your head to a corner — dodge. You can't hold a corner longer than 3 seconds
03Throw a hand UPWARD to punch — raise it high for more reach toward the rival
04A steady hand touching your own head parries — but only in your own half. A stretched-out or moving hand won't parry
05Punching a parry costs YOU 1 damage and stuns that hand for 2 seconds. Dodging opens the puncher up for 1 second — free hit! If both punches collide mid-air, both hands get penalized
065 hits in a row stuns you for 3s (can't cover). 9 in a row is a knockdown — pump either hand up 3 times fast to get up (each earlier knockdown makes it harder)
073 rounds, 60 seconds each. Damage meter starts at 100 — 3 knockdowns in one round ends the fight. Survive a round and both fighters heal +20 HP
08Keep both hands visible to the camera, above your head, and roughly in front of your shoulders — a lost, low, or flared-out hand goes dead (and stays dead for 2s after the camera finds it again)
09Go the distance without a KO and it's decided on points — damage landed, plus bonus points for every knockdown scored
